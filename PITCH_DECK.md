  3. **Why Existing Tools Fail:** Generic chatbots hallucinate or illegally provide stock tips; no unified tool validates screenshots, URLs, and claims in one place; non-English and senior investors are ignored.

### Slide 4: Specialized Feature Breakdown
- **Title:** Six Specialized Verification & Protection Engines
- **Modules Covered:**
  - **A. Screenshot Analyzer:** OCR + heuristic scanning extracts text from forwarded images and flags scam patterns instantly.
  - **B. URL Safety Checker:** Inspects domains for IDN homograph punycode (`xn--`), broker typosquatting, port exploitation, and HTTP traps.
  - **C. Claim Verifier:** Regex-audits SEBI 12-character registration numbers (`INH`, `INA`, `INZ`, `INP`) and enforces statutory bans on guaranteed returns.
  - **D. Content Classifier:** Calculates Promotional vs. Educational scores, flagging VIP funnels, urgency triggers, and penny stock hype.
  - **E. Regional Voice AI:** Voice-in, Voice-out assistant with slow speech rate for seniors, delivering vernacular safety verdicts.
  - **F. Resilience Test:** Practical simulation with 8 Indian fraud scenarios, generating verifiable investor safety badges.
- **Speaker Script (45 seconds):**
  > *"Our architecture features 6 specialized micro-engines: a Screenshot OCR Analyzer for forwards, a URL Checker that catches Cyrillic punycode lookalikes of brokers like Zerodha and Groww, a SEBI Claim Verifier that audits statutory prefixes like INA and INH, a Promotion Classifier that detects hidden funnels, a Senior-Friendly Voice Assistant, and a real-world Resilience Simulator."*

### Slide 7: User Journey & Operational Workflow
- **Title:** From Suspicious Message to Verified Protection in 5 Seconds
- **Five-Step Flow:**

  2. **Submit:** Investor snaps a screenshot, pastes the text or link, or speaks directly into the Voice Assistant.
